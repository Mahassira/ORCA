Our Company

Moving Trade. Connecting Markets.

ORCA Shipping and Agencies, established in 2022 in Egypt, has quickly positioned itself as a trusted leader in the maritime and logistics industry. Serving as the official agent for several renowned shipping lines, ORCA delivers a full spectrum of shipping and logistics solutions tailored to the needs of global trade. Backed by a team of seasoned professionals and industry experts, the company ensures seamless operations, efficient execution, and service excellence while upholding the highest international standards.
